Medhansh Tewary portfolio (v8)

To publish: put every file and folder from this zip into the ROOT of your GitHub repo
(index.html must sit at the top level, not inside another folder). Replacing the files
updates the same Vercel link.

What is new in v8
- The blue theme is gone. The whole site is now black dominant, with bone white type and one
  deep red accent. Bone paper appears only as occasional "break" sections (Work, Manifesto, Translation).
- Every surface carries a fine texture: soft clouds on black, paper fibre on bone, a halftone dot field on the red
  closing section, plus a light film grain over everything. Strong enough to feel material, quiet enough to read.
- Many more animations, on purpose:
  rolling button and link text, magnetic buttons, scrambling mono labels, a scroll progress bar,
  a cursor spotlight with a comet trail, click ripples and shards, letters that narrow and glow near the cursor,
  tilt and glare on posters and gallery images, highlighter underlines that draw in, speed-based skew on big text,
  slot machine counters on About, parallax numerals and a spinning badge, a five strip page curtain between pages.
- The About story section now pins and plays as four chapters as intended (this was a bug in v7).

Smoothness pass (v8.1)
- Lighter cursor effects, the film grain and spotlight no longer blend over the whole screen, effects only run for what is on screen,
  and the About 3D scene tunes its own sharpness to keep a steady frame rate on slower machines.
- Scrolling is a touch more responsive.

Notes
- Some EP gallery images load from Google Drive. Replace them with local files in assets/work/ when convenient.
- EP.04 Social Media is a placeholder until material is added.
- On phones the cursor effects switch off, the About blob responds to touch and, where allowed, to tilt.
- Visitors who prefer reduced motion, or whose browser has no WebGL, get a plain readable version of About.
- You can double-click index.html or about.html to preview the design locally, but YouTube refuses to play embedded videos from a folder, so the Motion clips only play from the live link (or any real web address). Locally they show their thumbnail with a note.
- Motion clips start muted when the section is reached and resume when hovered. If a clip ever cannot play, it shows a Tap to watch prompt; tapping opens it full screen with sound.
- research-brief.md lists the sites studied for this design.
