Who I am

I'm a graphic designer who treats every frame like a small argument. It has to make its point fast, to someone who was not planning to listen.

Since 2019 I have delivered 200+ creatives for founders, brands and people with strong opinions about fonts. The tools keep changing. The question never does: what will make a stranger stop?

01 Context

CON-
TEXT
FIRST.

Before I open a file I ask who is looking, where they are scrolling and what else is fighting for their thumb. A poster is never alone. It lives between a notification and an ad, so I design for that gap.

02 Form

FORM IS
THE
STORY.

Type is a voice. Colour is a mood. A crop is an argument. I let the form say the thing, so the caption has less to do and the viewer has more to feel.

03 Culture

Anime panels, football terraces, brutalist posters, a sandal on hot pavement. I collect what people already love and point it somewhere new. Templates are for people with time to be forgettable.
